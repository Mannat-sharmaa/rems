2. User Roles & Account Eligibility

REMS operates on a role-based architectural model. You agree to provide accurate and truthful identification upon registration:

  • Property Owners: Must possess legal title and authority to sell, lease, or rent the listed properties without undisclosed liens or encumbrances.

Customers may book physical tours of properties through the platform. By scheduling a visit:

  • You agree to arrive on time at the designated property coordinates.

  • Abusive booking patterns, repeated no-shows, or harassing behavior towards agents will lead to immediate account suspension.

5. Booking Transactions & Concurrency Policy

REMS employs strict Database ACID (Atomicity, Consistency, Isolation, Durability) transaction protocols:

  • Double-Booking Prevention: When a customer submits a booking, the property is locked via database row-level locking (`SELECT ... FOR UPDATE`). Only one transaction can successfully reserve the property.
  • Status Transitions: Upon successful transaction confirmation, an automated database trigger updates the property status from Available to Booked or Sold, ensuring real-time consistency across all user dashboards.
  • Binding Nature: An online token booking reserves the exclusive negotiation and acquisition right for a standard duration of 14 calendar days, pending final deed execution.

6. Fees, Token Deposits & Refunds

Token payments made through the portal serve as an earnest money deposit:

  • All digital payments (UPI, Cards, Net Banking) generate an automated cryptographic transaction ID and printable receipt.
  • If a seller fails to produce clean title deeds within the reservation window, the token deposit is 100% refundable to the customer.
  • If a buyer arbitrarily withdraws from an agreed purchase without legal cause, cancellation fees may apply as per the bilateral agent-customer agreement.
